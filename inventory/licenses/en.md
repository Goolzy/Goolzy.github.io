## 📜 Open Source Licenses

Inventory is built on the open source software and fonts listed below. Our thanks to every copyright holder and community involved.

> ⚠️ **This page is supplementary information, not a replacement for the legal notices.**
> The notice texts each license requires are **shipped inside the app itself** — the `NOTICES` bundle Flutter generates at build time (the full license text of every dependency) and the `OFL-*.txt` originals of the five bundled fonts. This page is a human-readable summary of that content.
