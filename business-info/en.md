## Scope of Business

Goolzy is a one-person media content business that researches and develops digital games and gaming-related content.

- Development and operation of mobile/desktop applications
- Curation of game and gaming content information
- Providing information about external stores through affiliate marketing (we do not process payments or sell directly)

---

## Affiliate Disclosure

This site and the apps we operate participate in affiliate programs including Coupang Partners, Epic Games Support-A-Creator, Humble Partner, Fanatical (CJ Affiliate), Green Man Gaming, GOG (Adtraction), Linkprice, Impact and Ubisoft Creator, and **may receive a commission on purchases you make at external stores**. Payment, delivery, refunds and exchanges at those stores are handled under each store's own policy; Goolzy is not a party to those transactions.

This disclosure is made under the Korean "Act on Fair Labeling and Advertising" and the Korea Fair Trade Commission's "Guidelines on Endorsements and Testimonials".

## Dispute Resolution

For questions, reports or disputes related to our services, please contact us at the customer support number or email above. We aim to respond within 7 business days.

If a consumer dispute cannot be resolved, the following organisations can help under the Korean "Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce":

- **Korea Consumer Agency** (tel. 1372 / [www.kca.go.kr](https://www.kca.go.kr))
- **Electronic Commerce Mediation Committee** ([www.ecmc.or.kr](https://www.ecmc.or.kr))
- **Korea Fair Trade Commission** ([www.ftc.go.kr](https://www.ftc.go.kr))
